# Customer Repeat Purchase Analysis — Insights

## Key KPI
- Total customers: **4,312**
- First-time customers: **1,419 (32.91%)**
- Repeat customers: **2,893 (67.09%)**
- Repeat purchase rate: **67.09%**

## Business Insights
1. The repeat purchase rate of **67.09%** shows that repeat buying is a major part of the customer base.
2. Repeat customers are more numerous than first-time customers, indicating comparatively strong customer loyalty.
3. The first-time segment is the key retention opportunity. A second-purchase campaign can convert one-time buyers into repeat customers.
4. Repeat customers can be encouraged through loyalty rewards, personalized product recommendations, bundles, and re-engagement offers.
5. The Python workflow also calculates **AOV (Average Order Value) by segment** automatically when Quantity and UnitPrice/Price are present in the source dataset.
